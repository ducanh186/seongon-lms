# SEONGON Learning

SEONGON Learning is a course platform with distinct public browsing, student learning, and administration experiences.

## Product surfaces

**Public Site**:
The customer-facing course and news website available to Guest, Student, and Admin.

**Admin Portal**:
The management surface available only to Admin. It excludes Student learning and purchasing controls.

**My Courses**:
The Student-only surface containing enrolled, active, and completed courses.

## Roles

**Guest**:
A visitor without an authenticated account.

**Student**:
An authenticated learner who can own a cart, enroll, learn, complete courses, and download certificates.

**Admin**:
An authenticated manager who can access the Admin Portal. Admin is not a Student.

## Navigation

**Courses Trigger**:
The desktop header item labelled `Khóa học`.

**Mega Menu**:
A desktop category panel opened by hovering over or focusing the Courses Trigger.

**Guest Cart**:
A visible cart entry point that asks a Guest to sign in before using Student cart functions.

## Content

**Generated Course Image**:
A locally stored, course-related illustration produced with `imagegen`.

**Generated Testimonial**:
Fictional testimonial content created for demonstration and visibly identified as illustrative content.
